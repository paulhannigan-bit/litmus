# PRD: AI Meeting Assistant

## Overview
We will build an AI-powered meeting assistant that leverages cutting-edge large language models to revolutionize how teams collaborate. The assistant will seamlessly integrate into users' workflows and unlock unprecedented productivity.

## Problem Statement
Meetings are often unproductive. Users struggle to keep track of action items and key decisions. There is a significant opportunity to improve the meeting experience with AI.

## Goals
- Improve user productivity
- Increase engagement
- Delight customers
- Drive revenue growth

## Target Users
All knowledge workers who attend meetings.

## Features
1. Automatic meeting summaries
2. Action item extraction
3. Sentiment analysis of participants
4. Real-time translation into 40 languages
5. Smart follow-up email drafting
6. Calendar optimization suggestions
7. Integration with Slack, Teams, Zoom, Google Meet, Jira, Asana, Notion and Salesforce
8. AI coaching on speaking time
9. Searchable meeting archive

## User Stories
- As a user, I want summaries so that I can save time.
- As a manager, I want insights so that I can make better decisions.

## Success Metrics
- User satisfaction
- Adoption rate
- Accuracy of summaries

## Technical Considerations
We will use a state-of-the-art LLM. The engineering team will determine the best architecture.

## Timeline
Launch all features in Q1.

## Risks
AI may occasionally make mistakes. We will mitigate this with robust testing.
