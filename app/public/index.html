Litmus

Know if your AI-assisted PM work is good enough before anyone else reads it.

AI makes drafting fast, and it makes generic, unconvincing drafts just as fast. Litmus tests your PRD, prototype or eval plan against 60 standards set by product leaders, tells you whether it passes, and gives you the fixes that matter most.

01 · PASTE

Drop in your draft

A PRD or spec, an AI-feature eval plan, or a prototype described by its screens or build prompt. Litmus works out which one it is.

02 · TEST

It’s checked against expert standards

20 standards for each kind of document, drawn from interviews with 52 product leaders. That includes the mistakes AI-written drafts make most.

03 · FIX

Get a verdict and what to change

A score against a 75% pass mark, an AI slop check, and your top three fixes rewritten for your draft. Each fix links to a clip of the expert who set the standard.

What is it?

Expert standards sourced from The Growth Podcast transcripts. Every expert quote is checked word for word against its source; transcripts are machine-made, so check the clip before you republish a quote. Experts are credited as the source of a standard. They have not reviewed Litmus or your draft.
